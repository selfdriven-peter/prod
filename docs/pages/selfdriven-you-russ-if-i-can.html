If I can change,
you can change.

I was screwing up a lot. I'm not going to give you the whole story, because this page isn't about me. It's about what I'm running now, so you can run it too.

Nobody's coming to sort it out for you. I waited on that for years. The engine's already in you. You just have to look inside and start it.

01 · Where most of us start

When you've got nothing, you can't see past today.

When the rent's due and the fridge is empty, you're not thinking about five-year plans. You're thinking about tonight. That's not you being weak or dumb. That's survival mode, and anyone would be in it.

So the first job isn't a big dream. It's getting one foot out. A few dollars of your own. One thing finished. One good day. You feel different after that, and you start thinking different too.

Nipsey Hussle talked about survival mode like this. That's the idea that hit me hardest.
02 · What I run now

Four gears. No rules.

selfdriven has four gears in it. Here's how I'd say them to you, if we were sitting in the car.

Curious

Pick who you listen to.

I stopped taking advice from people who've got nothing I want. Find someone who came from where you are and got out. Then work it backwards. What did they do first? What did they do second?

Try it todayName one person who started with nothing. Look up what their very first step was.
Caring

What you put out comes back.

Energy's a boomerang. If all I put out is anger, that's what comes back, just in a different shape. So I watch three things: what I wake up thinking, what I say, and what I do. And caring counts for yourself too.

Try it todayCatch one thing you were about to say that you wouldn't want coming back at you. Don't say it.
Constructive

A plan kills doubt.

If all you've got is a dream, the first problem looks like the end. If you've got a list of steps, a problem is just a problem, and you still know the next step. You don't build a wall. You lay one brick, every day.

Try it todayWrite down three steps toward the thing you want. Do the smallest one before tonight.
Chill

It's a marathon.

Nothing worth having happens overnight. When it feels heavy and uncomfortable, that doesn't mean you're failing. It means you're stretching. Stay in your own lane, there's no traffic in it. And resting is part of the run. It isn't quitting.

Try it todayWhen it gets heavy, stop for fifteen minutes. No phone. Then go again.
03 · Every morning

Say it out loud. Every morning.

Nipsey ran a little script with his daughter on the way to school, every single day, until she knew it without thinking. This is mine. Say each answer out loud before you tap it. Out loud is the point.

04 · One brick a day

Build your wall.

Nobody builds a wall in a day. You lay a brick. One thing done, then put it here. Keep going and one day you look up and there's a wall. Nobody sees this but you. It stays on this phone.

Typing's a pain? Tap the box, then the mic on your keyboard, and just say it.

05 · Pass it on

Then it's your turn to be the proof.

Someone showed me this, and now I'm showing you. That's the deal. When you've got a few bricks down, send this page to one person who's where you were.

Look up to me if it helps. But the real point is, one day someone's going to look up to you.
