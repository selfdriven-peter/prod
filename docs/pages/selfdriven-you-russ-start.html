Talk, don't type

Do this bit first, because it changes everything else on the page. You can talk to all of it instead of typing, and you can make all of it read itself out loud to you.

And the big one: Claude does not care about spelling. Type it how it sounds. It works out what you meant and never mentions it. No red squiggles, no one looking over your shoulder.

Week one

Claude

It's a thing you talk at. It talks back. It's not a person and it's not your mate, and it won't fix anything on its own. What it is: there at 3am, doesn't get bored, doesn't tell anyone, and has no idea what you did last year.

Don't test it with something clever. Ask it something you actually want to know.

How to talk to it

Like a mate who happens to know things. Say what you actually want, and tell it when it's lost you. "I don't get that, say it simpler" works every single time.

What it's not

Not a doctor, not a lawyer, not always right. If it matters, check it. If it's about a person, talk to the person.

stopping counts as doing something

When you're stuck

You will get stuck. Everyone does, including me, most days. Stuck isn't a sign you're not cut out for this. It's just where the next bit of learning is sitting.

One section per sitting is plenty. This isn't a race and there's no prize for doing it all in one night.

In this order
  1. Fifteen minutes is the limit.After fifteen minutes of it not working, stop pushing. Past that is where laptops get thrown.
  2. Screenshot it. Paste it into Claude."What does this mean and what do I do?" It has seen every error message ever written.
  3. Still stuck: text Pete the screenshot.Just the picture. No essay needed. Or save it for the call, if it can wait.
  4. Then leave it. Come back tomorrow.It'll still be there and you'll be fresher. Stopping counts as doing something.
Nothing here can be broken

Not properly. Accounts get recovered, laptops get reset, pages get deleted and made again. Worst case on this page is a wasted afternoon, and you've had those before.
